# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Tunisian university & high-school students**: Supplementing curriculum or preparing for exams/bac, often accessing content via mobile phone on 3G/4G or intermittent connections.
- **Job seekers & career switchers**: Seeking practical tech, AI, software, and professional skills who cannot afford commercial bootcamps or subscription LMS platforms.
- **Community educators & creators**: Local volunteers, educators, and AI-content curators publishing structured, open educational tracks in Tunisian Arabic (Derja), French, and English.

## Product Purpose

Dars (درس) is a 100% free, community-driven, open-source MOOC platform built to democratize quality education in Tunisia. It hosts structured AI-generated and community-curated courses (video, text, code, interactive quizzes) and is engineered to run at $0/month on Cloudflare's serverless edge with progressive offline PWA capabilities.

## Brand Personality

- **Empowering & Community-Owned**: Warm, encouraging, accessible to everyone regardless of socio-economic background.
- **Crisp & Focused**: Fast, distraction-free learning environment that respects the learner's time and device data plan.
- **Culturally Rooted**: Native bilingual Tunisian experience (Tunisian Arabic / Derja with full RTL support, French, and English).

## Anti-references

- **Bloated Corporate LMS**: No cluttered Moodle/Canvas-style visual noise, nested menus, or multi-step clunky bureaucracy.
- **SaaS Dark-Mode Slop**: Avoid saturated neon-on-black crypto/AI aesthetics or generic generic gradient cards.
- **Heavy Data Hog**: Never force uncompressed auto-playing high-bitrate video or megabyte-heavy JS bundles on mobile learners.

## Design Principles

1. **Lightweight & Offline-First**: Every screen must feel instantaneous on 3G. Lessons and progress cache gracefully in the PWA.
2. **Dual-Script Harmony**: Native RTL Arabic typography (Tajawal/Cairo) sits with equal balance and dignity alongside Latin typography (Plus Jakarta Sans).
3. **Intentional State Over Decoration**: Visual cues (completed, in-progress, revision needed) carry clear meaning without decorative distraction.
4. **Sea Blue Serenity with Minimal State Red**: Mediterranean sea blue drives trust and focus; Tunisian crimson is strictly reserved for live signals, urgency, and active badges.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum compliance with high-contrast text ratios for readability in bright sunlight.
- Comprehensive screen-reader and keyboard navigation support.
- Low-bandwidth asset negotiation and full offline storage support.
